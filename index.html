Why AidFirst exists

My grandfather passed away two months ago. A caregiver missed the signs. Not knowing made us reluctant to act.

He was 85, had Parkinson's, and had suffered multiple brain strokes. One night his breathing became gritty and labored. The caregiver assured us everything was fine. We believed her. He passed away that night.

AidFirst is built for that moment — when someone is sick, you're scared, and you don't know what to do. It won't diagnose. It will help you understand how serious the situation is, in plain language, in your language, and tell you exactly what to do next.

How it works

Three steps. Any language. Any phone.

No medical training needed. No English required. Just describe what you see.

Features

Built for the real world

Every feature exists because someone, somewhere, needed it to exist.

🌐
Multilingual
Understands 140+ languages. Responds in Hindi or English. Recognizes Hinglish, broken grammar, and panicked short sentences.
🎤
Voice input
Upload a voice memo in any language. Whisper transcribes it and Gemma 4 reasons over it — no typing needed in a crisis.
📷
Photo analysis
Send a photo of the patient. AidFirst observes visible symptoms — pale lips, skin color, swelling — and factors them into the assessment.
📵
Offline ready
Runs entirely on-device using Gemma 4 E4B. No internet required after setup. Works in villages, remote areas, anywhere.
🔊
Audio responses
Every response is read aloud in the caregiver's language. Because in a panic, reading is hard. Hearing is easier.
⚠️
Emergency detection
Confirms multiple danger signs before escalating. Never calls emergency on a single symptom. Bilingual alert with immediate instructions.

Built on Gemma 4

Using every capability the model was designed for — vision, audio, multilingual reasoning, agentic questioning — in one unified pipeline.
